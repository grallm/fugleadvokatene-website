We are volunteers in Oslo and Bergen who rescue and rehabilitate urban birds. We do this alongside our jobs and other commitments.

We want to build understanding for the birds we share our cities with. Every individual matters.

## Would you like to help?

We need both practical help and financial support. [Find out about volunteering](/en/volunteer/) or [see how you can support us](/en/support/).
